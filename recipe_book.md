# Lamb Soup (and Turtle Soup!)

**Told by:** Elizabeth E. Lee

## Ingredients

- 2 lbs Lamb (shank, shin, or stew meat, cut into 2-inch pieces) – *To taste*
- 1 large Onion – *chopped*
- 2 Carrots – *chopped*
- 3 Celery stalks – *finely chopped* 
- 1/2 lb Sweet Potatoes - *small, cubed*
- 2 quarts Water – *Enough to cover the meat*
- 1/2 lb Veal Shanks - *cut into pieces – 2-inch pieces*
- 2 tbsp Flour – *to thicken*
- 1 tsp Dried Thyme - *a pinch*
- Salt and Pepper - *to taste* 
- ½ Cup Brown Sugar - *for sweetness* 
- Butter – *about 2 tbsp* 
- Wine – *A half pint*

## Steps

1.  Scrub the Lamb, cut it into 2-inch pieces. In a large pot, wash the lamb in cold water for 30 minutes – this removes any gamey flavors.
2.  Add the chopped Onion, Carrots and Celery to the pot with the Lamb. Pour in the 2 quarts of water.  Bring to a boil, then reduce heat to a simmer. Cover and simmer for about an hour – or until the meat is almost falling off the bone.  Stir occasionally. 
3.  Add the diced Sweet potatoes and the Veal Shanks to the pot.  Simmer for another hour, or until they begin to soften. 
4.  In a small bowl, whisk together the Flour and Brown Sugar.  Slowly stir the mixture into the simmering broth, creating a slurry.  (Let it simmer for a few minutes).
5.  Add the thyme. (Simmer for 15-20 minutes) 
6.  Check the thickness of the broth - add a little water if needed. 
7.  Remove the Veal Shanks from the soup. Cut the meat from the bone, placing in a hot pan and cover with butter and brown syrup. 


## Tips from the Cook

*   Don’t rush the simmer—that's the key to a tender lamb!* 
*   For a really rich flavor, add a bay leaf when you add the vegetables.* 
*   You can add small bits of cooked mushrooms at the end for a little color and earthy flavor.
*   Simmer this soup for 2–3 hours while it’s coming together– it really takes.
* **Turtle Soup’s secret**– adding a generous amount of water while making the soup is what gives it that special depth.*

Enjoy!

# Corn Fritters

**Told by:** Elizabeth E. Lee

## Ingredients

*   1 pound Green Corn, cut in 3-4 inch pieces
*   1/2 cup Flour
*   1/4 cup Milk
*   2 Eggs, beaten
*   1 tbsp Butter
*   1 tsp Pepper
*   1 tsp Salt
*   About 1-2 Cord of Corn Pulp


**Steps**

1.  Peel and wash the green corn.
2.  Put the corn in a pot large enough to cover it completely.
3.  Pour in enough water to submerge the corn. Bring to a boil and let it boil for about 30 minutes, or until the corn has softened.
4.  Once the corn is softened, cut it into smaller pieces like pieces about 1 inch long. 
5.  Pour a spoonful of the corn mixture into a bowl.
6.  Add the beaten eggs and mix them in
7.   Stir in the butter, salt and pepper and mix it in.
8.  Deep fry the corn mixture in a pan or on a hot stove.  About 3 minutes.
9.  Turn the corn pieces and gently push them over with a spatula. This makes it uniform in size.
10. Keep the fritter warm in the oven to avoid the food getting soggy. 

**Tips from the Cook**

*   Always keep your corn fresh by letting it dry thoroughly before storing. It makes a little difference when using them in fritters to ensure the flavour of this dish remains fresh and delightful. 
*   Don’t overcrowd the pan while frying! This will prevent the corn from frying evenly. Fry in batches if necessary. 
*   Small amounts go a long way! It is often better to add small amounts rather than a giant batch. 
*   Remember, little corn can serve many times! It’s a simple recipe that really is satisfying.
